How the auction works

  1. One NFT at a time. Each Miner NFT is auctioned individually using BID tokens.
  2. 30-minute opening. A new NFT auction starts with a 30-minute countdown.
  3. 2-minute countdown. After the first valid bid, the countdown becomes 2 minutes.
  4. Reset on every new highest bid. Each subsequent valid highest bid resets the countdown to 2 minutes.
  5. Winner. The highest bidder wins the NFT when the countdown reaches zero.
  6. Refunds. If you are outbid, your previous bid becomes a refundable balance that you can claim.
