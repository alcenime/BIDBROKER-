04NFT Auctions and Bidding

Miner NFTs are distributed through auctions, not a fixed-price mint.

  • Maximum NFT supply is 5,555.
  • One NFT auction runs at a time, and NFTs are auctioned individually.
  • Each new auction begins with a 30-minute countdown.
  • After the first valid bid, the countdown changes to 2 minutes.
  • Each subsequent valid highest bid resets the countdown to 2 minutes.
  • When the countdown reaches zero without another valid bid, the highest bidder wins.

Bids are blockchain transactions and are binding once confirmed.

05Locked Bids and Refunds

The current highest bidder's bid remains locked. If you are outbid, your previous bid becomes refundable. You must claim your refundable balance before reusing those funds for another bid. Refundable balances are separate from tokens designated for burning.
