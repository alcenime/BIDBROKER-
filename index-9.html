How It Works

Three simple steps to participate in BIDBROKER.

STEP 01

Bid to Mint

Join the auction and place bids using BID tokens. Each Miner NFT is auctioned individually, and the highest bidder wins when the auction ends.

STEP 02

Outbid, Refund & Burn

Your bid remains locked while you are the highest bidder. If another user outbids you, your previous bid becomes refundable. Claim your refund before using those tokens to place another bid. BID tokens that remain unused for the auction are burned according to the protocol rules.

STEP 03

Win & Mine ETH

Win a Miner NFT and activate mining to earn ETH rewards according to the protocol's mining rules and available reward pool.

  • Maximum NFT supply is fixed at 5,555.
  • Only one NFT auction runs at a time.
  • Each new NFT auction begins with a 30-minute countdown.
  • When the first valid bid is placed, the countdown switches to 2 minutes.
  • Every subsequent valid new highest bid resets the countdown to 2 minutes.
  • If the countdown reaches zero without another valid bid, the highest bidder wins the NFT.
  • After an auction ends, the next NFT auction begins with a fresh 30-minute countdown.
  • Outbid users can claim their refundable balances, and must do so before using those funds for another bid.
  • BID tokens designated as unused auction tokens are burned according to the protocol rules. Refundable balances owed to users are never burned.
  • Bid principal, refundable balances, and any separate bid fee are distinct values.

01Introduction

BIDBROKER is an NFT auction and ETH mining ecosystem on Robinhood Chain. Miner NFTs are won through auctions that use the BID token. Holders can then put their Miner NFTs to work to earn ETH rewards.

This document describes the confirmed mechanics of the project. Economic parameters that have not been finalized are marked To be announced or To be specified and should not be treated as commitments.

02How BIDBROKER Works

  • Bid. Users place bids on Miner NFTs using BID tokens.
  • Win. Each NFT is auctioned individually. The highest bidder when the countdown ends wins the NFT.
  • Mine. Winners can use their NFTs for mining and receive rewards paid in ETH.

BID is the bidding currency. ETH is the reward asset. The two are kept separate throughout the protocol.

03NFT Auction Mechanics

Locked bids and refunds

A bid stays locked while its owner is the highest bidder. When another user outbids them, the previous bid becomes a refundable balance that the user can claim. Users must claim a refund before reusing those funds for another bid.

Refundable balances belong to the user. They are not the same thing as tokens designated for burning, and they are never burned.

04BID Token & Burn

BID is used to place bids. Tokens designated as unused auction tokens are burned according to the protocol's defined rules.

  • Refundable outbid balances are never burned.
  • The winning bid principal is not assumed to be burned.
  • Bid principal, refundable balances, and any separate bid fee are distinct values and are not assumed to be equal.

07Supply & Limitations

  • Maximum NFT supply is fixed at 5,555.
  • One auction at a time, with NFTs auctioned individually.
  • Maximum of four NFTs per wallet can be used for mining.
  • Mining boost tops out at 5x with four NFTs.
  • Reward availability is limited by the ETH reward funds.
